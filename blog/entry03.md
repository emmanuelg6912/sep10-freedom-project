### Content 

#### 1. using 3d printing to help in creating models of animal habitats and wildlife

I had come up with three specific future inventions that I believed could help in advancing animal consevation. The first Idea that I had come up with was **using 3d printing to help in creating models of animal habitats and wildlife.** How the invention would work is that first a realistic 3d model could be printed out and created. Next a group of scientists or engineers can use these models to construct a larger real life habitat within the wild allowing for more conservation of habitats and in researching animal population growth. The use of 3d printing can be cost effective allowing for more models to be created without it being so pricey. 3d models dont always need to involve wildlife but can also be used in marine conservation. 3d printed coral reef models can be implemented into different oceans allowing for maintaining life cycles and populations within marine wildlife. 

#### 2. Using radio frequency signals 
Radio signals can be implemented into specific areas of animal wildlife which can allow for further communication between different conservationalists in further regions who need help or resources depending on the current situation. For example, an SOS signal can be sent from vietnam to zimbabwe allowing for zimbabwe to give resources to the region depending on the situation thats occuring. Another thing to note is that if possible, radio transmissions sent to near countries can be translated in specific languages by just picking up the signal first then fully translating the language based on where the signal is being sent. 

#### 3. Using different materials that automatically camouflage in specific enviornments
How this camouflage material would work is that a button can be pressed from a software that allows the material to scan out its enviornment and uses the enviormental colors to create an rgb gradient that can automatically camoflauge with its enviornment. Camera traps and drones would be a good idea to implement these materials because of their small design that could make it more easier to implement these materials but it may be costly depending on what type of material is used. However, these camoflauging materials are reusable and can be used over and over again without breaking.
