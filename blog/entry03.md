The first Idea that I had come up with was **using 3d printing to help in creating models of animal habitats and wildlife.** How the invention would work is that first a realistic 3d model could be printed out and created. Next a group of scientists or engineers can use these models to construct a larger real life habitat within the wild allowing for more conservation of habitats and in researching animal population growth.
