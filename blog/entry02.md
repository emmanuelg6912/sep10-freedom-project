### Content 
I first decided to research more into past software advancements made in Animal Conservation and I found a lot of surprising results. When I searched up **"animal conservation technology apps"** found a lot of animal conservation apps that would often use **user support** to help in gathering data on animal wildlife populations and their movements. However one that stood out to me the most was the website called [eBird](#) which is an app that allows users to take pictures of wildlife and share that data to other users of the app. This sharing of images can allow scientists to receive more data and analytics on the locations of bird species, their populations, and their migration patterns. This information can provide new opportunities for future developments in animal conservation and can also allow scientists to take action to ensure that animal wildlife is protected. Another website that I found was called [wild track](#) which is an app that specializes in the use of data analytics, artificial intelligence, and ecological knowledge to help in preserving wildlife.
