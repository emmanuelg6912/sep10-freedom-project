#### Researching for past software advancements 
I first decided to research more into past software advancements made in Animal Conservation and I found a lot of surprising results. One thing I realized was the importance of being more specific in my searches. When I searched up "animal technology apps", and "animal data apps" I had found no results. However, when I searched **"animal conservation technology apps"** I had a greater amount of searches and websites that gave me more in depth information. In most of the websites I reseached I noticed a lot of animal conservation apps that would often use **user support** to help in gathering data on animal wildlife populations and their movements. However one that stood out to me the most was the website called [eBird](https://ebird.org/home) which is an app that allows users to take pictures of wildlife and share that data to other users of the app. This sharing of images can allow scientists to receive more data and analytics on the locations of bird species, their populations, and their migration patterns. This information can provide new opportunities for future developments in animal conservation and can also allow scientists to take action to ensure that animal wildlife is protected. I then found another website called [wild track](https://www.wildtrack.org/) which is an app that specializes in the use of data analytics, artificial intelligence, and ecological knowledge to help in preserving wildlife. One thing that was really interesting to me about this app however was how accessible it was to the public and its reliable software technologies. One part of the app that really showed this was another app that was included named **FIT/Footprint Identification Technology** which uses photography on footprints of animals to help in tracking the data of animal population with just a simple picture of a footprint. With these non invasive methods data collecting can be alot more easier, less invasive to animal territories, and can further develop software advancements in animal conservation. One last website I found for software advancments was [Earth Ranger](https://www.earthranger.com/) which has more of a focus on gathering data and analytics on animal habitats and their enviornment. One thing that really surprised me about a part of the website called the Ecoscope. The ecoscope is  free open source model that uses **GPS tracking** to track animal locations and their time spent on specific areas. Because of these trackings rangers can locate species of animals that are in the presence of poachers and can calculate populations of animal species.
