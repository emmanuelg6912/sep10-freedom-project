# Entry 7
##### 5/26/26

### Context
I am currently a student who is in the SEP10 program which is a program in my school that is meant to teach students the fundamentals about computer science. Students in 10th grade will learn about html and css fundamentals while also learning about the implementation of an IDE to keep information and files organized. And in 11th grade students will learn more about javascript and a focus on how to make videogames. During the SEP10 program, students engage in a project called the freedom project which makes students choose a topic they are passionate about, research it, and using that research to create a website that lists current software and hardware advancements made and future advancements that could be made from your project. after That is done, students present their websites to their classmates and present their websites in a school EXPO showcase. This blog entry will show my takeaways for my in class project presentation and my expo elevator pitch showcase aswell as showing sources to my presentation plan and presentation

### content
#### takeaways for in-class-presentation
My presentation was mainly about animal conservation and It went over current and future software and hardware advancements that could be made in animal conservation through my website and I also explained the challenges I faced when reaching my MVP and my overall takeaways. The feedback I was given alot of the time was to improve more on the size of text within my slides and make it more visible to readers. I would often notice that sometimes my text was either really big or small and sometimes images would end up taking too much space.
