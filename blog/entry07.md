# Entry 7
##### 5/26/26

### Context
I am currently a student who is in the SEP10 program which is a program in my school that is meant to teach students the fundamentals about computer science. Students in 10th grade will learn about html and css fundamentals while also learning about the implementation of an IDE to keep information and files organized. And in 11th grade students will learn more about javascript and a focus on how to make videogames. During the SEP10 program, students engage in a project called the freedom project which makes students choose a topic they are passionate about, research it, and use that research to create a website that lists current software and hardware advancements made and future advancements that could be made from your project. After that is done, students present their websites to their classmates and present their websites in a school EXPO showcase. This blog entry will show my takeaways for my in class project presentation and my expo elevator pitch showcase as well as showing sources to my presentation plan and presentation

### Content
#### takeaways for in-class-presentation
My [presentation](https://github.com/emmanuelg6912/sep10-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/presentation.md) was mainly about animal conservation and It went over current and future software and hardware advancements that could be made in animal conservation through my website and I also explained the challenges I faced when reaching my MVP and my overall takeaways. The feedback I was given a lot of the time was to improve more on the size of text within my slides and make it more visible to readers. I would often notice that sometimes my text was either really big or small and sometimes images would end up taking too much space. I also needed to improve more on my speed/time of the project. I would often spend more time than needed talking about my website and It took me almost 15 minutes for me to finish my presentation. Next time, I should make sure to keep my presentation short, and try to customize it more to make it seem more appealing to the audience.

#### takeaways for Expo elevator pitch
The main piece of feedback that I would give to myself is on maintaining my confidence in the expo elevator pitch. I noticed that sometimes I would often slowly lower my voice or freeze for a few seconds when I'm giving my elevator pitch mainly because of the time constraints that I'm given. I did also notice that I did take a little more longer than usual which was over 2 minutes but my pacing was a lot more efficient then my in-class-presentation which did make me more confident later during the expo. Next time, I should make sure to be confident in my presentation and keep a constant loud and clear volume instead of lowering my voice during the presentation and make sure to be quick with my elevator pitches at least up to a minute or two.

### EDP/Engineering Design Process
I am currently at the last step of the engineering design process being that I now have to present my results to an audience. I've ended up presenting my product to my classmates meaning that the engineering design process is complete! However, using my feedback I had gained from my presentation, during the break I may take steps to change my website and improve it more.

### Skills
Some skills I needed to learn in order to be successful was **consideration**, **creativity**, and **communication**.
1. consideration was an important skill that I needed to keep in mind. During my presentation I would have to think about what impacts that my animal conservation inventions may bring and questions that came up during my presentation were usually a question about how my invention might impact the world so being able to consider possible problems and impacts was important during my presentation.
2. creativity was another important skill I took into consideration. I needed to be able to come up with an idea that could prove to be interesting or different compared to other projects in my class, so that during my presentation or expo, someone will be more interested in my topic and will be more engaged in the presentation.
3. communication was the last skill that was the most important overall. A main piece of feedback I was given to myself was to work on my volume when communicating to others about my project. I would often be loud and clear at first but then my volume would lower more and more and then go back up. So, being able to maintain a constant volume can allow me to communicate more clearly about my project and be able to be more successful when presenting my project
