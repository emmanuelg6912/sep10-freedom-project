part A/current software and hardware advancments

eBird

eBird functions as an app that can track bird populations through the use of user input(taking pictures of birds) that can help in maintaining bird populations.

The ability for users to take pictures can help in gaining data on flight patterns of certain bird species. This in return provides opportunities for developments in the future


Earth Ranger

Earth Ranger is used as a data tracking app which is used to track data from animals, habitats, and environments to help in protecting wildlife(NOTE: this app is only acsessible to rangers so other people who are intereste in animal conservation may not be able to use this app).


wild track

wild track also functions as a data tracking app similar to that of earth ranger with the difference being that pictures of animal footprints can be taken to allow for easy treacking of animals.

this feature functions through the use of the FIT/Footprint Identification Technology which allows for simple tracking and data of animal populations by just taking a picture of an animals footprints


Enviornmental dna

uses the gathering of natural samples to reveal new and unknown species within the wild

This process however can also be inefficient due to its large amount of steps and its cost that may not be acsessible to all parts of the world.


tagging and collaring/biologging

an electronic tag is attached to an animals body. This attachment can help in tracking their movement while also provinding analytics to animal movement patterns and also can protect animals from danger

however, this hardware requires sedation of the animal which can disturb them.

camera traps

camera traps are recording devices that track animal movements within an area without the disturbance of wildlife. This allows for the discovery of new animal species and populations that can be secured more easily.

camera traps are also cheap for obtaining but getting advanced cameras can be costly going up to 500 to 2000 dollars


drones

drones in animal conservation can be used to scan out models of landscapes which could in return be used to track which animals and species inhabit specific areas therefore allowing for more data on animal populations and possibly new ones aswell.
